## 最終プロンプトセット

各プロンプトは下記の共通指定の [scene] を場面指定に置き換えたものです。

```
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: portrait mobile cafe game exploration background, no UI. Draw [scene] Style: Japanese cozy retro cafe game, hand painted watercolor and gouache children's picture book, softly outlined shapes, visible paper grain, carefully composed charming natural scenery, sophisticated muted colors, no photorealism. Portrait 1024 by 1536. Keep central lower half calm open grassy earth for interactive gather objects to be overlaid. Maintain readable contrast behind cream UI at top and bottom. No people, animals, buildings, food, baskets, signs, lettering, text, frames, grid or UI. Full bleed illustration.
```

- clearing: A sunlit forest clearing with a winding cream trail splitting towards the upper left and upper right. Leafy trees frame the edges. Ferns and tiny flowers along the margins. Warm sage green, butter sunlight, earthy brown. Open grass in the lower middle.
- river: A quiet forest riverside with a narrow turquoise stream along the right edge, a curved stepping stone path leading towards the upper middle. Trees, grassy bank, mossy stones frame the scene. Sage green and muted teal, warm natural sunlight. Open grassy earth in the lower middle.
- spring: A secret moonlit woodland with a luminous pale turquoise pond in the upper middle, old tree roots, tiny white flowers and subtle glowing edges. Muted blue, sage green and warm gold fireflies. Daytime-readable, friendly and welcoming rather than dark. Open grassy earth in the lower middle.

## 実装と確認

- ForestScreen：風景内の採集対象・行き先の道標、上部の体力と空き枠、下部の帰還・かご・手帳。
- ForestSheet：採集方法、発見結果、かご、切れ端、帰還結果を下から開く。閉じられない保留結果では受け取り/置く操作が必要。
- スマホサイズ 390 × 844、360 × 780、320 × 568 で採集・移動・かご・切れ端・帰還を実際に操作。320 × 568 のHUD折り返しと、満杯時の入れ替え一覧に決定ボタンが重なる問題を修正。
- 体力0時の無料帰還、2枠食材を受け取る際の空き不足、深部の解放条件は既存の状態処理と画面テストで確認。
- 探索・抽選・体力回復・保存処理は変更していません。保存の回帰検証は既存ゲームテストを使用。今回のブラウザ再読込では探索復元を確認できず、実機での再読込成功は記録していません。
